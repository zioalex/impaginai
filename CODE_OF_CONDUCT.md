# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rule is short: **be decent to people.**

In practice, for issues, pull requests and discussions here:

- Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it. "This loses the user's
  overrides when the disk is full" is useful; "this is amateur work" is not.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not. Often they do.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no comments about anyone's identity, and
  nothing you would not say to a colleague you respect.
- Contributions of photographs, captions or albums must be yours to share.
- Reports of a security problem get handled privately — see
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove anything that breaks this, and may
block an account that keeps doing so. If something here makes you
uncomfortable, message the owner through
[github.com/zioalex](https://github.com/zioalex).

This is written in its own words rather than adopted wholesale; the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
says the same thing at greater length and is worth reading if you want the
longer form.
