Thank you for contributing. This agreement lets the Project Owner distribute
your contribution under the project's open-source licence (AGPL-3.0-only)
**and** under commercial licences, without asking you again each time. **You
keep the copyright in your contribution.** You are granting a licence, not
handing the work over. In return, section 3 promises that what is published
publicly stays source-available.

## Definitions

**"You"** is the individual or legal entity that submits a Contribution. If You
submit on behalf of a legal entity, "You" means that entity together with its
Affiliates, and the individual who signs confirms they are authorised to bind
it.

**"Affiliate"** is any entity that controls You, is controlled by You, or is
under common control with You, where control means holding more than half of
the voting rights or the equivalent power to direct the entity.

**"Contribution"** is any code, documentation, template, lab specification,
design, translation or other work that You intentionally submit to the Project,
in any form and by any means — a pull request, an issue, a patch sent by
message or email. A work that You conspicuously mark in writing as **"Not a
Contribution"** is excluded.

**"Project Owner"** is Alessandro Surace (GitHub:
[zioalex](https://github.com/zioalex)), the individual who owns this
repository, and any person or entity to which this agreement is assigned under
section 7.

**"Project"** is this repository, any repository that succeeds or replaces it,
and the software, services and products the Project Owner builds from them.

## 3. The open version stays open

Where the Project Owner publishes to the general public a version of the
Project that contains Your Contribution, that version will be published under
the AGPL-3.0-only, under another licence approved by the Open Source
Initiative, or under a licence that gives the public the right to read, use,

The Project Owner is not obliged to publish any version, to carry on
publishing, or to publish on any particular schedule, and may offer the same

Project Owner does with code of their own.

You confirm that:

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, and unless You state

You are not obliged to provide support. The Project Owner is not obliged to
use, keep or accept any Contribution, and is not obliged to pay You.

## 6. Credit

The Project Owner will credit You in the Project's contributors list and will
not misrepresent who wrote a Contribution. This is a courtesy, not a condition
of the licences You grant, and it does not oblige the Project Owner to preserve
any particular file, commit or revision history.

## 7. Assignment

The Project Owner may assign or transfer this agreement, and the licences and
rights granted under it, in whole or in part and without Your consent —
including to a company the Project Owner forms, and to anyone who acquires the
Project. Whoever takes the assignment also takes on sections 3 and 6. You may
not transfer Your obligations under this agreement without the Project Owner's
written agreement.

## 8. Scope, and later versions of this agreement

This agreement covers every Contribution You make to the Project, before and
after You sign it. The licences You grant for a Contribution cannot be
withdrawn once that Contribution is submitted; You are of course free to stop
contributing at any time.

The Project Owner may publish a later version of this agreement. A later
version applies only to Contributions submitted after it is published, and
never changes the licences already granted.

## 9. General

This agreement is governed by Swiss law, excluding its conflict-of-law rules.
So far as the law allows, the courts at the Project Owner's place of residence
in Switzerland have exclusive jurisdiction; mandatory rules that give You a
different court, as consumer-protection rules may, are unaffected.

If a clause turns out to be unenforceable, the rest still applies, and the
unenforceable clause is read down to what the law does allow. This agreement,
together with the licences in the repository, is the whole agreement about
Contributions. It creates no employment, agency or partnership between us. An
agreement given electronically, by the comment below for example, counts as
given in writing.

## 11. Open points for legal review

Not part of the agreement — a note of what a Swiss IP lawyer should still
settle. Delete this section once it is settled.

1. **The forum clause in section 9.** Name the actual court rather than "place
   of residence", and check it against ZPO Art. 35 for contributors who count
   as consumers, and against the Lugano Convention for contributors domiciled
   in the EU.
2. **How far moral rights can be waived** in section 1 under URG Art. 11, and
   whether consent should be more specific about renaming and about proprietary
   distribution.
3. **Section 4.4 on AI-assisted work.** Disclosure is one approach; the
   alternative is to say nothing and rely on 4.1 and 4.3. Decide which risk you
   prefer.
4. **The third limb of section 3**, which is there so a later move to a
   time-delayed licence such as BSL or FSL stays possible. Drop it if you want
   to commit to OSI-approved licences only.
5. **Whether to split this into separate individual and entity agreements**, as
   Apache does with its ICLA and CCLA, if companies start contributing.
6. **Fairness review under UWG Art. 8**: the grant is broad and unpaid, which
   is normal in the industry, and section 3 is the consideration that balances
   it. Worth confirming.
